# Prose examples

## Banal abstraction

Spine:
The sentence tries to report an operational change.

Main faults:
- Banality: generic nouns hide actor, action, and effect.

Representative contrast:

Checked via:
Not run

Weak:

> The initiative represents a meaningful step toward improving stakeholder outcomes through enhanced collaboration.

Fault:

No subject acts. `Initiative`, `meaningful step`, `stakeholder outcomes`, and `enhanced collaboration` hide who does what and what changes.

Better:

> The support team now triages billing disputes before renewal calls, so account managers stop discovering blocked renewals in the meeting.

Why:

The revision names the actor, action, object, timing, and consequence. It replaces decorative abstraction with a causal relation.

## False force

Spine:
The sentence tries to report a committee decision.

Main faults:
- Weakness: the adverb claims force while the verb avoids decision.

Representative contrast:

Checked via:
Not run

Weak:

> The committee decisively moved forward with a comprehensive review of possible options.

Fault:

The force sits in `decisively`, but the verb phrase only says the committee began to think.

Better:

> The committee rejected the vendor proposal and reopened the build option.

Why:

The action now carries the force. No adverb has to pretend a review is a decision.

## Distant cause

Spine:
The sentence tries to explain a release delay.

Main faults:
- Obscurity: the cause follows the effect.
- Weakness: passive voice hides the actor.

Representative contrast:

Checked via:
Not run

Weak:

> The release was delayed after several issues were found in the payment flow by QA during regression testing.

Fault:

The actor and cause arrive after the delay. Passive voice hides the sequence.

Better:

> QA found three regression failures in the payment flow, so the team delayed the release.

Why:

Cause precedes effect. The reader does not have to reconstruct who discovered what and why the release moved.

None

QA found three regression failures in the payment flow, so the team delayed the release.
